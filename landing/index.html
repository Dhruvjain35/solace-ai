Architecture

A serious clinical AI stack

FastAPI on AWS Lambda (arm64), CloudFront + WAF + API Gateway, DynamoDB with CMK encryption + 30-min PHI TTL, AWS HealthScribe under BAA for ambient capture, Claude Sonnet 4.5 for reasoning, LightGBM 5-fold ensemble + SHAP for triage.

1

Capture

Browser MediaRecorder + iOS native AVAudioRecorder. KMS-encrypted S3. Diarized in HealthScribe with role-aware speakers.

2

Reason

Two-stage prompting: structured fact extraction with utterance citations, then Solace-style narrative. SHAP + conformal sets surface uncertainty.

3

Act

Notes, problems, allergies, vitals push to the EHR via SMART-on-FHIR. CDS Hooks injects suggestions. Letters render to PDF. SMS goes out in the patient's language.

Trust & safety

The boring slide CMIOs care about

Solace is built for procurement scrutiny. We publish model cards. We publish accept/edit/reject metrics. We never auto-submit clinical writes. Conformal sets give calibrated uncertainty so clinicians know exactly when the model is unsure.

  • 1Public model cards — every Solace AI ships with a card covering training data, performance, limitations, fairness, governance. HTI-1 DSI ready.
  • 2Override audit log — every accept / edit / reject gets logged per clinician, per model. Accept-rate dashboards are public for procurement.
  • 3Conformal prediction sets at 90% and 95% coverage on triage and Ddx — calibrated uncertainty no other vendor publishes.
  • 4HIPAA-ready posture — KMS-encrypted DDB, 30-minute PHI TTL, redacted CloudWatch logs, JWT clinician auth, WAF rate limits. AWS BAA in progress.
  • 5No autosubmit — Solace surfaces suggestions. The clinician signs every write to the chart.

FAQ

Common questions

Does Solace need an Epic Workshop / App Orchard listing? ↓

Not to evaluate. The free demo and the group tier work today against OpenEMR (Docker) and against any SMART-on-FHIR sandbox (we ship adapters for Epic, Oracle Health, Athena). Listings come when a hospital wants Solace deeply embedded in Hyperdrive — Solace already implements SMART App Launch v2 with PKCE and JWT backend service auth, so the Showroom paperwork is a process step, not a code step.

What about the FDA? ↓

Solace is decision support, not a medical device. Every AI write requires clinician sign-off (the FDA's CDS human-in-loop carve-out under the Sept 2022 Final Guidance). We never auto-submit notes, orders, or coding. We publish model cards and accept-rate dashboards per HTI-1 DSI transparency.

Is patient data safe? ↓

PHI is encrypted at rest with KMS, in transit with TLS 1.3, has a 30-minute local TTL, and never appears in CloudWatch (we install a UUID/Bearer redaction filter at boot). The AWS BAA covers HealthScribe, Bedrock, S3, DynamoDB, and Lambda. AWS BAA is in progress; until it lands, ambient scribe runs against synthetic transcripts in production.

How long does deployment take? ↓

For solo and group: minutes. Sign in, point at your EHR sandbox or production OAuth, start using. For a health-system deployment with custom workflows + branded patient intake: 2-4 weeks. The no-code workflow builder lets your operations team configure most of it themselves.

Why is Solace cheaper than Abridge or DAX? ↓

Two reasons. (1) We anchor on AWS HealthScribe (covered under the AWS BAA at a flat $0.10/min) instead of building our own ASR. (2) We ship the operations + scribe + reasoning stack in one product, so you don't pay $300/clinician/month for the scribe and another enterprise contract for prior auth and another for intake.
